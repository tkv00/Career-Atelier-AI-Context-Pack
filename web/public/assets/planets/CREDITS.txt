Planet and sky textures for Career Atelier

Original maps: Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Earth daymap, Earth clouds,
Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Sun, and Stars + Milky Way.
Downloaded from https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/.
Changes: planet maps resized to 1024 x 512; Milky Way map retains its
original 2048 x 1024 resolution. All maps converted to WebP.
Lighting, animation, Saturn's rings, and the graph layout are rendered by Career Atelier.

These textures are visualization maps, based partly on NASA observations
and supplemented by Solar System Scope. The experience graph uses artistic
positions and sizes; it is not an astronomical scale model.
